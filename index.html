Approach

Start with the decision that needs to be made.

ER Squared does not begin with a platform, a product, or a predetermined solution. The work starts with the business objective, the constraints, the stakeholders, and the consequences of getting the decision wrong.

From there, the path becomes practical: clarify the problem, identify the options, align the people who matter, and move toward an outcome that can actually be executed.
